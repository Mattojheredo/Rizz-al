Education & Life

Before he was a national hero, Rizal was a curious boy, a top student, and a young doctor. Trace the schools, teachers, and cities that shaped his mind, from a small home in Calamba to the universities of Europe. Each stage shows how learning became the foundation of his fight for reform.

The Story of His Life

❦
  1. 1861

    Born in Calamba

    José Rizal is born on June 19 in Calamba, Laguna, the seventh of eleven children.

  2. 1872

    The Execution of the Gomburza

    At age 11, he sees the country shaken by the execution of three priests, an event that deeply marked his family and his view of injustice.

  3. 1882

    Leaving for Spain

    He secretly sails to Spain to continue his studies, beginning his years in Europe.

  4. 1884–1885

    Doctor in Madrid

    He completes his licentiate in Medicine and then his studies in Philosophy and Letters at the Universidad Central de Madrid.

  5. 1887

    Noli Me Tángere

    His first novel is printed in Berlin and soon makes him a voice for reform, and a target.

  6. 1891

    El Filibusterismo

    His second novel is printed in Ghent, Belgium, darker and angrier than the first.

  7. 1892

    La Liga Filipina and Exile

    He founds a reform society in Manila and is exiled to Dapitan days later, where he works as a doctor and teacher.

  8. 1896

    Bagumbayan

    On December 30, at age 35, he is executed by firing squad, after writing his farewell poem, Mi Último Adiós.
