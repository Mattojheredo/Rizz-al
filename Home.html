A WEBSITE ABOUT JOSÉ RIZAL

"There is no tyrants where there are no slaves." — José Rizal

José Rizal was a Doctor, Writer, and National Hero. His novels Noli Me Tángere and El Filibusterismo exposed the abuses of Spanish rule and helped inspire the movement for Philippine independence. This blog goes through his life, his works, and the ideas he left behind.

Rizal at a Glance

  • Born June 19, 1861, in Calamba, Laguna
  • Noli Me Tángere published in 1887
  • El Filibusterismo published in 1891
  • Executed December 30, 1896, at age 35
