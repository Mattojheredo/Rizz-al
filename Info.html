My Reflection

❦

Nung simula ng project na ito, I only wanted to make a website about kay jose rizal that did not look like a boring school report. In the end, I made Rizz-al (the website's name hehe) a website with four pages such as Home, Education & Life, Works, Developer's Info, Headers and Footers, I used colors like old paper, Brown with some golds so it looks like an old book and konting vintage hehe. My favorite part of making this is probably the Footers and Developer's Info hehe, I was so happy making it and actually took my time coding it instead of rushing it hehe it tooks me hours of hours sa dalawang yan.

Making this website helped me learn more about Rizal. I had to read the three major literary works nya such as Noli Me Tángere, El Filibusterismo, and Mi Último Adiós and search about them on the internet, So i understood them better. I also saw how much he gave up to speak against unfair treatment.

I also learned more on how to build a website it really taught me alot and now im more consistent kesa sa dati. Sa HTML i learned footers and typewrite, sa CSS mas natutunan ko pano mag-grid cause hirap na hirap ako dun dati, sa Javascript the sliders and scroll things.

The hardest part was debugging. There's so many times where my page is so broken and looked broken even though it's working naman. A photo was on top of the text, the footer was in the wrong place, di gumagana scrolls and sliders. Most of the time, the problem was small tas di ko parin makita kung san yung errors tas missing closing tag, file name or wrong capital letter lang naman pala yung mali. I also learned to check the browsers for errors, and i learned to be patient, and yeah yun lang sana nagustuhan nyo huhu.

Matt Jhared Miole
